PATIENT INFORMATION

Your Guide to a Comfortable Healthcare Experience

Everything you need to know before, during, and after your visit to a healthcare facility.

WELCOME TO MEDIBRIDGE

Information for Our Patients

Visiting a healthcare facility should feel clear and comfortable. At MediBridge, we want you to know what to expect before you arrive, while you are with us, and after you leave.

This section covers how to prepare, book an appointment, what to bring, how visitors can support you, and answers to common questions.

BE PREPARED

Before Your Visit

Prepare ahead to make your healthcare visit smoother and more comfortable.

01

Confirm Your Appointment

Check the date, time, and the doctor or service you booked.

02

Gather Your Medical Information

Bring a list of your current medications, known allergies, and past conditions or surgeries.

03

Prepare Your Questions

Write down your symptoms, when they started, and anything that worries you so nothing is forgotten during the consultation.

04

Follow Special Instructions

Some tests need fasting or other preparation. You will be informed in advance if yours does.

05

Arrive Early

Come 15–20 minutes before your appointment for registration and check-in.

06

Wear Comfortable Clothing

Wear comfortable clothes that are easy to adjust for examinations.

YOUR HEALTHCARE JOURNEY

Appointment Guide

Follow these simple steps to help you navigate your healthcare appointment.

01

Choose Your Service or Doctor

Browse our Services and Doctors pages to find the right care.

02

Book Your Appointment

Contact us through our phone number, email, online booking form, or visit our front desk.

03

Receive Your Confirmation

Your schedule will be confirmed through SMS, email, or a phone call.

04

Check In Upon Arrival

Present your ID and booking details at the registration desk.

05

Meet Your Care Team

Your doctor or technician will attend to you at your scheduled time.

06

Follow Up

After your visit, you will receive your results, prescriptions, or advice on your next steps.

Need to reschedule or cancel?

Please let us know at least 24 hours ahead so another patient can take your slot.

WHAT TO BRING

Required Documents

Make sure to prepare the following documents before your visit.

01

Valid Government ID

Bring a valid identification card for verification.

02

Appointment Confirmation

Bring your appointment confirmation or reference number.

03

Health Insurance or HMO

Bring your insurance or HMO card, if applicable.

04

Doctor's Referral

Bring your referral or request form, if you were referred.

05

Previous Medical Records

Include previous medical records, laboratory results, or imaging films related to your concern.

06

Medication List

Prepare a list of your current medications.

For emergency patients:

Emergency patients will be attended to first. Documents can be completed afterward.

NEED HELP?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about your healthcare visit.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended for consultations, laboratory tests, imaging, and checkups. Emergency care is available without an appointment.

What if I am running late?

Please call us as soon as possible. We will try to accommodate you or help you reschedule.

Can I bring someone with me?

Yes. A companion is welcome, subject to our visitor guidelines.

How will I receive my test results?

Results are released through pick-up, email, or the patient portal. Your doctor will explain them to you.

What payment options do you accept?

Payment options may include cash, credit or debit cards, and HMO or insurance. Please confirm coverage with your provider before your visit.

What should I do if I feel worse before my appointment?

If your symptoms become severe or you think it is an emergency, go to the Emergency Department or call your local emergency hotline right away.
