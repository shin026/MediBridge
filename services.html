Diagnostic Imaging

A clearer view of your health.

Diagnostic imaging uses medical technology to examine structures inside the body and help identify possible health conditions. Common procedures include X-rays, ultrasound, CT scans, and MRI, depending on the facility's available equipment and services. These examinations assist doctors in evaluating and diagnosing medical conditions.

Preventive Care

Prevention is an important part of staying healthy.

Preventive care focuses on maintaining good health and identifying possible health concerns before they become more serious. It includes routine checkups, health screenings, vaccinations, and lifestyle guidance. Regular preventive care helps individuals monitor their health and take appropriate steps to reduce health risks.

Medical Testing

Laboratory Services

Reliable testing to help understand your health.

Laboratory services involve medical tests that help detect, monitor, and evaluate different health conditions. These may include blood tests, urine tests, and other laboratory examinations requested by healthcare professionals. Test results help doctors make accurate assessments and recommend appropriate treatment.

Our Laboratory Services provide accurate and reliable testing to help assess your overall health and identify possible medical conditions. We offer a range of laboratory tests performed by trained professionals using modern equipment and proper testing procedures to ensure dependable results.

Available Laboratory Tests

Explore some of the common laboratory examinations used to help evaluate different aspects of health.

01

Blood Tests

Help evaluate overall health and detect possible health conditions.

02

Urinalysis

Examines urine to check for signs of infection and other health concerns.

03

Fecalysis

Analyzes stool samples to identify possible digestive problems or infections.

04

Blood Chemistry

Measures different substances in the blood to assess organ function and general health.

05

Hematology Tests

Evaluate blood cells and help detect conditions such as anemia.

06

Pregnancy Test

Detects pregnancy through a urine or blood sample.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Our Services?

Find answers to common questions about accessing healthcare and laboratory services.

What healthcare services does MediBridge provide?

MediBridge provides healthcare information and guidance about emergency care, diagnostic imaging, preventive care, and laboratory services.

How can I access your healthcare services?

You may explore the services on our website and contact us through our contact page for inquiries, assistance, or appointment-related concerns.

Do I need an appointment before visiting?

Appointment requirements may vary depending on the service. We recommend contacting the facility beforehand to confirm availability and requirements.

What should I bring during my visit?

Bring any necessary identification, medical records, previous laboratory results, and doctor's requests relevant to your visit.

What laboratory tests are available?

Common examinations may include blood tests, urinalysis, fecalysis, blood chemistry, hematology tests, and pregnancy tests, depending on availability.

Where can I ask additional questions?

You may visit our Contact page to find information on how to reach us and send your inquiries.
